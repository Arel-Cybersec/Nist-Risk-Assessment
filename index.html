    Method & sources
    
  
    
    
    
      
    Method
    
      
      
        
    • Scope: Terraform for AWS. It targets LocalStack by default and deploys to a real account with use_localstack = false. No real customer data.
      • 
        
    • Scoring: likelihood and impact on the five qualitative levels of NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1 (Very Low → Very High). SP 800-30 presents its risk tables as exemplary, to be tailored by each organization; this assessment uses a 5×5 score (likelihood × impact) mapped to those five levels. Ratings are the assessor's judgment, with the rationale recorded per finding.
      • 
        
    • Evidence: scan.py, OPA/Rego policies (conftest) and Checkov , run against the vulnerable baseline and the hardened code.
      • 
        
    • Runtime: GuardDuty, 28 AWS Config rules, a daily drift check and weekly backup restore tests cover the live account once deployed.
      •
